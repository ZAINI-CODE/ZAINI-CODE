## About

Hi, I'm **Muhammad Zain**, a web developer who enjoys building things that work well from end to end, from the interface people click on to the model running behind it. Most of my time goes into **React, Next.js and FastAPI**, and the rest into the AI side of things: **ML/DL, LLM fine-tuning, computer vision and multi-agent systems**.

I hold a **BSCS from Lahore Garrison University**. Its foundation in Computer Software Engineering gave me strong technical skills and experience leading development teams. I care about efficiency and good collaboration, and I like building practical, domain-specific solutions, especially in **automotive technology**.
